📖 Currency Glossary

TermDefinition
Appreciation An increase in the value of a currency relative to another
Base Currency The first currency in a currency pair (e.g., EUR in EUR/USD)
Central Bank Institution managing a country's monetary policy and currency supply
Depreciation A decrease in the value of a currency relative to another
Fiat Currency Government-issued currency not backed by a physical commodity
Forex Foreign exchange market where currencies are traded
Inflation Rate at which prices increase and purchasing power decreases
Legal Tender Currency that must be accepted for payment of debts by law
Liquidity How easily a currency can be bought or sold without affecting price
Pip Smallest price increment in forex (typically 0.0001)
Quote Currency The second currency in a currency pair (e.g., USD in EUR/USD)
Reserve Currency Currency held by central banks for international transactions
Spread Difference between the bid and ask price
Stablecoin Cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value
Volatility Degree of price variation over time
