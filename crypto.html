₿ Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual currency that uses cryptography for security and operates on decentralized networks, typically based on blockchain technology.

Key Characteristics

  • Decentralized — No central authority (bank or government) controls it
  • Transparent — All transactions are recorded on a public ledger
  • Immutable — Once recorded, transactions cannot be altered
  • Pseudonymous — Users identified by wallet addresses, not names
  • Limited Supply — Many cryptocurrencies have capped supply (e.g., Bitcoin: 21 million)

How Cryptocurrency Works

  1. Blockchain — A distributed ledger recording all transactions
  2. Mining/Validation — Computers solve complex problems to verify transactions
  3. Wallets — Software/hardware storing private keys to access your crypto
  4. Exchanges — Platforms to buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies
💡 Did You Know? Bitcoin was created by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto, whose true identity remains unknown.

Stablecoins

Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies pegged to stable assets like USD (e.g., USDT, USDC). They aim to combine crypto benefits with price stability.
