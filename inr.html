🇮🇳 Indian Rupee (INR)

PropertyDetails
Symbol₹
ISO CodeINR
Subunit1 Rupee = 100 Paise
Central BankReserve Bank of India (RBI)
First Issued1540 (modern form: 1947)
Hindi Nameरुपया (rupayā)

History

The word "rupee" comes from Sanskrit "rūpya" meaning "wrought silver." The first rupee was introduced by Sher Shah Suri in 1540. After independence in 1947, India continued using the rupee with new designs.

The modern ₹ symbol was adopted in 2010, designed by D. Udaya Kumar. It combines the Devanagari letter "र" (ra) and the Latin letter "R."

Denominations

Coins: 50 paise, ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, ₹20

Banknotes: ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹2000

💡 Note: In 2016, India demonetized ₹500 and ₹1000 notes overnight to combat black money. New ₹500 and ₹2000 notes were introduced.

Key Facts

  • India has the world's fifth-largest economy
  • INR is a partially convertible currency
  • Digital payments (UPI) have grown massively in India
  • The RBI is exploring a Digital Rupee (e₹)

Currency Features

  • Notes feature Mahatma Gandhi on the front
  • Each denomination has a different color and theme (monuments, wildlife)
  • Notes include Braille features for visually impaired users
  • 17 languages appear on each banknote
