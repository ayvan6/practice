🌍 Major World Currencies

These are the most traded and economically significant currencies in the world, often called "reserve currencies" because central banks hold them in their foreign exchange reserves.

Currency Code Symbol Country/Region Central Bank
US Dollar USD $ United States Federal Reserve
Euro EUR € Eurozone (20 countries) European Central Bank
British Pound GBP £ United Kingdom Bank of England
Japanese Yen JPY ¥ Japan Bank of Japan
Swiss Franc CHF Fr Switzerland Swiss National Bank
Chinese Yuan CNY ¥ / 元 China People's Bank of China
Australian Dollar AUD A$ Australia Reserve Bank of Australia
Canadian Dollar CAD C$ Canada Bank of Canada
Indian Rupee INR ₹ India Reserve Bank of India

Why These Currencies Matter

  • USD — World's primary reserve currency; used for most international trade
  • EUR — Second most traded; used by 340+ million people
  • GBP — Oldest currency still in use; London is a major financial hub
  • JPY — Key currency in Asian trade; often used as a "safe haven"
  • CHF — Known for stability; Switzerland's neutrality makes it attractive
💡 Did You Know? The US Dollar is involved in approximately 88% of all foreign exchange trades worldwide.
